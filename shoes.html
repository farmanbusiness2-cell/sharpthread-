Buying Tip

When shopping for everyday shoes online, always look for breathable materials (like mesh) and a non-slip sole. If you'll be walking long distances, prioritize cushioning and shock absorption over style. And remember: if you're between sizes, it's usually safer to size up slightly, especially for sneakers.
