Buying Tip

For a first watch, pick a 38–42mm case size — it fits most wrists comfortably. Stick to a simple dial and a neutral strap (black or brown leather, or stainless steel). You can always upgrade to a bold piece later. Also check that the watch lists its water resistance rating — even 30m is enough for daily handwashing.
